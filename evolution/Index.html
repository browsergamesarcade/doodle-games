How to play

Your goal

You start as a teeny tiny cell. Eat, grow and evolve all the way to the Top of the Pond!

Eat & grow

Munch creatures your size or smaller. They look worried when you get close! Every snack fills your XP bar, and a full bar means a level up.

Watch out!

Creatures bigger than you have grumpy eyebrows and pop a ! when they chase you. Bumping into them hurts. The face in the corner is your health, so keep it smiling! You heal a little over time and every time you eat.

Evolve

Some level ups make you bigger, and some let you pick a mutation: a new trick like swimming faster, biting harder or hiding better. Simpler mutations are listed first, just like real evolution. Mutations can keep evolving into stronger versions, so try different picks each game and find your favourite combo!

Score

Every snack scores points, and the faster you play, the bigger your bonus. Make it all the way to the Top of the Pond for a big speed bonus, then a 5-second feeding frenzy to squeeze in every last point!

Beat the top 3 (or your own best) and pop in your 3 letters. Your creature shows on the leaderboard, with a crown if you made it to the Top of the Pond!

Controls

Phone

Hold & drag anywhere. You swim towards your finger, and the further you drag, the faster you go. Tap a card to pick a mutation.

Desktop

Hold the mouse to swim towards it, or use WASD / arrow keys. Press the number keys to pick a mutation card, and Enter or Space for the big button.

The restart button (bottom left) asks "sure?" first, so tap it twice.

Top of the Pond!

Your score is locked in! Keep munching just for fun, or play again.
