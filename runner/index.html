How to play

Your goal

Your little runner never stops running! Keep up with the screen and see how far you can go.

Jump

Hop over holes and up onto walls. Hold jump a bit longer for a bigger jump, or just tap it for a little hop.

Sprint

Sprint for a quick burst of speed. You zoom ahead of your spot, and jumps from a sprint go higher and further, perfect for big gaps and tall walls! Your sprint needs a little rest before it's ready again, then you jog back to your spot.

Watch out!

Fall down a hole and it's game over. Bump into a wall and you'll stop, so jump over it quickly before the screen squishes you against the edge!

Score

Every metre you run counts, and the screen slowly gets faster the further you go. Beat the top 3 (or your own best) and pop in your 3 letters for the leaderboard!

Controls

Phone

Touch the right side of the screen to jump. Hold the left side to sprint.

Desktop

Space, W or Up to jump. Hold Shift, D or Right to sprint. Enter presses the big button.

The restart button (top right) asks "sure?" first, so tap it twice.
