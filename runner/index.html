How to play

Your goal

Your little runner never stops running! Keep up with the screen and see how far you can go.

Jump

Leap the gaps between buildings, and hop over (or onto) rooftop things like AC units, chimneys and stairwell huts. Hold jump a bit longer for a bigger jump, or just tap it for a little hop.

Sprint

Sprint for a quick burst of speed. You zoom ahead of your spot, and jumps from a sprint go higher and further, perfect for big gaps and tall walls! Your sprint needs a little rest before it's ready again, then you jog back to your spot.

Watch out!

Fall down a gap between buildings and it's game over. Bump into a wall or a rooftop thing and you'll stop, so jump it quickly before the screen squishes you against the edge!

Skill Cards

Run far enough and you'll find a Skill Card! There's a new one at 250, 600, 1000, 1500, 2100, 2800, 3600, 4500 and 5500 m, each with a new move and a little bit of gear. Once you have a move, bigger gaps and walls start to turn up!

Tricks

With the Trick card (and its backwards cap!), press sprint again in the air to flip, spin or cartwheel. You need lots of air: a sprint jump, or a jump down onto a lower roof. Land it for bonus points! Leave it too late and you'll tumble, and it takes a second to get back up.

Double Jump

With the Double Jump card (winged sneakers!), tap jump again in the air to jump once more. Hermes Wings give you a third jump!

Floaty Cape

With the Floaty Cape, hold jump while you're coming down to float gently over big gaps. Each jump gets about a second and a half of floating (more with the Super Cape).

Grapply Gripper

A grapple on your wrist! When a ring on a crane or on top of a tall wall goes ping, press grab to fire it. Crane rings swing you across big gaps (you let go at the top of the swing, or press jump to let go early). Wall rings pull you right up to the top. The Super Double one has a grapple on each arm, so you can grab the next ring while you swing!

Glasses of Awesomeness

So cool you can see further! With the shades on, the view zooms out a little so you can spot gaps and walls sooner.

Score

Every metre you run scores a point, and landed tricks score lots more. The screen slowly gets faster the further you go. Beat the top 3 (or your own best) and pop in your 3 letters for the leaderboard!

Controls

Phone

Touch the right side of the screen to jump. Hold the left side to sprint. Once you have tricks, touch the left side again in the air to do one. Tap the right side in the air to double jump, and hold it to float. With the Grapply Gripper, a grab! button pops up above sprint when a ring is in reach.

Desktop

Space, W or Up to jump. Hold Shift, D or Right to sprint. Once you have tricks, press sprint again in the air to do one. Tap jump in the air to double jump, and hold it to float. E (or S / Down) to grab. Enter presses the big button.

The restart button (top right) asks "sure?" first, so tap it twice.
